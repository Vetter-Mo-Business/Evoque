Understanding coaching

What is coaching?

Serious psychological coaching is aimed exclusively at healthy people, to support them with private or professional challenges. Within the coaching process, problematic behaviours that trouble the coachee and limit them on the way to their goals or in everyday life are worked through systematically. In coaching at EVOQUE, we look together at the root of the problem and create a lasting, healthy change in behaviour with the help of psychologically grounded and scientific methods.

More about the approach

Especially with long-standing issues, patterns and disruptive behaviours have often become entrenched over years. That is why it is important to address the cause of a topic before moving on to solving the problem. It is therefore central to allow enough time for a coaching process, in order to work through topics in depth and thus create a lasting change in behaviour. At EVOQUE, great importance is placed on tackling issues systematically. Clients should first understand and reflect on their own behaviour. This method takes time and practice, yet it is considerably more effective than simple tips.

The process

Your path to your goal

  1. 01

    Intro call

    We look at where your problem shows up. First we clarify how, when and why the behaviour exists.

  2. 02

    Root-cause exploration

    We look at where the behaviour might have originated. Understanding is the central basis for change.

  3. 03

    Setting goals

    We define your goal and the path towards it.

  4. 04

    Hands-on application

    We apply first practical exercises to gradually turn your old patterns into positive behavioural outcomes.

  5. 05

    Reflection

    Change is a continuous process. We reflect together and refine wherever things are not yet working.

  6. 06

    Lasting goal achievement

    Ideally, by the end of our coaching you will already have reached your goal. If not, you will have gathered all the tools along the way to keep exploring on your own.

What matters to me

In coaching I am your companion on your way to your goal. Together we create a goal plan that is realistic and achievable for you. For our work to succeed, my wish is that you bring a clear will to change something about your current situation.

Good to know

Coaching – not therapy.

How does coaching differ from psychotherapy?

Coaching is explicitly not suitable for the treatment or cure of psychotherapeutic conditions. Coaching does not explore the past of a problematic behaviour to a psychotherapeutic depth. Coaching is about changing the behavioural consequences in the here and now and shaping them into positive behavioural patterns that are useful to the coachee. Coaching can be used as a supportive measure alongside psychotherapy; this is clarified individually according to the situation. Should you be diagnosed with a relevant psychotherapeutic condition before or during the coaching process, I ask you to inform me without delay. Should such tendencies emerge over the course of the coaching, you will be advised accordingly.

Important note EVOQUE does not constitute therapy or medical treatment for therapeutic concerns.
